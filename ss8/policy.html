This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data when you use the AI Legal Support Platform for Travelers (“Platform”, “we”, “our”, “us”).

By using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Country of residence
  • Language preference
  • Account credentials

1.2 Legal & Case Information

  • Messages sent to the AI Legal Assistant
  • Uploaded documents
  • Case descriptions
  • Communication history related to legal assistance

1.3 Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Device and browser information
  • Usage logs
  • Cookies and similar technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Provide and operate the Platform
  • Deliver AI-powered legal guidance
  • Facilitate escalation to real legal professionals
  • Improve service quality and accuracy
  • Ensure security and fraud prevention
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. AI Processing

Information submitted to the Platform may be processed by AI systems to:

  • Analyze documents
  • Provide legal explanations
  • Generate recommendations
  • Prepare draft responses

AI outputs are informational only and do not constitute legal advice.

4. Data Sharing

We may share data:

  • With verified legal professionals when you request escalation
  • With payment providers to process transactions
  • When required by law or legal authorities

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored securely using industry-standard safeguards
  • Access is limited to authorized personnel only
  • Data retention is limited to what is necessary for service delivery and compliance

6. GDPR & User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request data portability

Requests can be sent to: privacy@[yourdomain].com

7. Cookies

We use cookies to improve functionality and user experience. You may control cookie settings through your browser.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of changes.
