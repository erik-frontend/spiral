What open banking

enables

Real-time settlement through local instant payment rails (Faster Payments, SEPA Instant, and equivalents): Instant Account-to-Account Payments

Confirm ownership and validity of user bank accounts to reduce fraud and ensure secure payouts: Account Verification

Users authorise a payment directly from their bank account through secure authentication — no card details required: Pay by Bank

Initiate outbound transfers to user bank accounts for withdrawals, refunds, and partner disbursements: Payout Initiation

Enable automated account-based payments for subscriptions, scheduled transactions, and ongoing services: Recurring Billing

Key benefits

for merchants

and

platforms

Eliminate card interchange and scheme fees on payment flows

Reduce fraud exposure — users authenticate directly with their bank

Improve checkout conversion with a frictionless, familiar bank login experience

Accelerate settlement timelines versus traditional card acquiring

Support compliance with KYC and AML requirements through verified bank account data

Available across UK, EU, and selected international markets via partner network
